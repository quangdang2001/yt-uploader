Privacy Policy

video-analytics · Last updated: 31 October 2026

video-analytics is a private tool operated by one individual. It uploads that person's own videos to YouTube channels they own. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

Only data in Google accounts owned by the operator, after access is granted through Google's OAuth consent flow. We use the Google Drive API to read the video, thumbnail and metadata files produced by our own pipeline and to move them after upload, and the YouTube Data API to upload the video and set its privacy status, scheduled publish time, synthetic-media declaration and thumbnail. No other Google service is accessed, and no account other than the operator's own.

Data we store

OAuth tokens for the operator's own Google accounts, stored encrypted on the operator's own machine. Video files are stored only while an upload is in progress and deleted afterwards. Logs of failed uploads are kept for at most 72 hours. We collect no personal data from anyone other than the operator.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through these APIs with any third party, and do not use it for advertising, profiling or training machine-learning models.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until revoked. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
